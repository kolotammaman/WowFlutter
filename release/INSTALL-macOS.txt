WowFlutter - install on macOS

1. Copy WowFlutter.component to   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
2. Copy WowFlutter.vst3 to        ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

These builds are not notarized by Apple, so macOS may refuse to open them the first time.
If a plug-in does not show up or macOS says it cannot be verified, open Terminal and run:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/WowFlutter.component
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/WowFlutter.vst3
